How a WellnessQuoin setup runs

  1. Import product sales and lead times

    Send 12 to 24 months of SKU-level sales and each supplier’s lead time.

  2. Read the inventory forecast

    Within a week you see 12 weeks of forecast demand and cover for every SKU.

  3. Approve the reorder plan

    Your buyer reviews each draft order, edits quantities and sends it to the supplier.

WellnessQuoin’s forecasting platform on AWS

The platform we are building. The public demo calls none of these services.

  • S3 and Lambda

  • SageMaker AI

  • Bedrock

    Plain summaries of seasonal changes for buyers

  • DynamoDB

    SKUs, suppliers, reorders and the approval log

Questions

What retailers and distributors ask before a forecast.

Ask about a forecast

Which sales data do you need?

Weekly or daily units per SKU, as CSV from your shop platform or POS.

Do you see customer details?

No. Sales are aggregated by SKU and week before forecasting.

What does it cost?

A subscription priced by the number of SKUs you plan. We quote after the first forecast.

Does it place orders?

No. It drafts reorders; your buyer approves and sends them.

Does it suit distributors?

Yes. Distributors plan by warehouse and customer group instead of store.
