# Product

WellnessQuoin: Intelligent merchandise planning for adult wellness.

AI demand planning software that helps adult wellness retailers forecast inventory requirements using aggregated sales records and supplier lead times.

- Founder and CEO: Oluwaseyi Babatunde
- Co-founder and CTO: Chisom Maduka
- Industry: SexTech, Retail, Supply Chain Management
- Customers: Sexual wellness retailers, distributors and specialized ecommerce businesses
- Core features: Demand prediction, stock replenishment, seasonal analysis, inventory aging, supplier planning
- MVP: Product sales imports and inventory forecasts
- Planned AWS: SageMaker AI, S3, DynamoDB, Lambda, Bedrock
- Business model: SKU-based subscriptions
- Main action: Request a forecast
